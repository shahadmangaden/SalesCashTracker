AgentRoute

Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version

AgentRoute is a tool for your own field-sales records. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your records are never sold or shared.

What the app stores

  • Your records: the agencies, contacts, visits, follow-ups and notes you enter. By default these stay on your phone only.
  • Optional cloud account: if you sign in (Google or email), your records are also saved to that account in Google Firebase so you can get them back on a new phone. You can sign out at any time.
  • Backups: backup files are created only when you ask, and saved wherever you choose.

Phone permissions

  • Location: used when you check in to a visit, save an agency's location, or sort today's route nearest-next. It is not tracked in the background.
  • Business-card scanning: the card is read on the phone by the Google Play services scanner; AgentRoute keeps only the text you save.
  • Notifications: follow-up reminders.
  • Network: syncing, if you use a cloud account, and opening maps.

Third parties

Google Firebase stores data for signed-in accounts. Google Maps opens when you choose Directions or Plan route. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone. To delete a cloud account and its records, email hello@agentroute.app and we'll remove it.
